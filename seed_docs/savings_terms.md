# Lantern Demo Bank: savings account terms

Fictional bank and products for demonstration only. Effective 1 October 2026.

The Lantern Everyday Savings account has a minimum monthly average balance of INR 1,000.00. The Lantern Starter Savings account has no minimum balance requirement. Savings accounts have no monthly maintenance fee.

Interest on Everyday Savings is 3.00 percent per year, calculated by the bank and credited quarterly. The assistant must not estimate interest or calculate a customer's return.

Customers may request a printed account statement. The fee schedule lists the charge. Account opening requires fictional proof of identity and address in this demo. Contact support for help with account terms.
