# Lantern Demo Bank: complaints and disputes

Fictional process for demonstration only. Effective 1 October 2026.

Report a disputed transaction to support within 30 calendar days of its statement date. Provide the transaction date and reference through the support channel; never provide your password or PIN.

Support acknowledges complaints within 2 working days and aims to resolve them within 10 working days. If an investigation requires more time, support sends a progress update.

If a complaint is unresolved, ask support to escalate it to the demo complaints officer. The assistant cannot file a dispute, reverse a transaction or promise a refund.
