# Lantern Demo Bank: loan and EMI FAQ

Fictional product information only. Effective 1 October 2026.

The demo personal loan has a fixed annual interest rate of 12.00 percent and a term of 12 to 36 months, subject to approval. The processing fee is INR 500.00. EMI means equated monthly instalment.

The bank supplies the repayment schedule after approval. This assistant must not calculate an EMI, estimate eligibility or recommend taking a loan. Contact support to request an official repayment schedule or discuss a missed instalment.

There is no early repayment fee on this fictional personal loan. Payments and loan applications cannot be submitted through the assistant.
