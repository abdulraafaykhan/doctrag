# Lantern Demo Bank: debit card FAQ

All information is fictional and for demonstration only. Effective 1 October 2026.

If a debit card is lost, contact Lantern support immediately to request blocking. This assistant can explain the process but cannot block cards or perform account changes.

Replacement cards normally arrive within 7 working days after support approves the request. The fee schedule lists replacement and annual fees.

The demo debit card has a daily cash withdrawal limit of INR 10,000.00 and a daily purchase limit of INR 25,000.00. PIN resets must be requested through support. Never share a PIN, password or verification code in chat.
